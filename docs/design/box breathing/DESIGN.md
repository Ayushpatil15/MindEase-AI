## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a calm sanctuary for academic life—balancing modern focus with restorative mindfulness. The visual language speaks to students experiencing stress, burnout, and cognitive overload, providing an intuitive, tranquil space free from clinical coldness, medical stigma, or corporate austerity.

### Tone & Sensibility
- **Empathetic & Non-Clinical:** Guidance is structured around gentle nudges, reflective pauses, and supportive check-ins rather than diagnostic metrics.
- **Rooted & Restorative:** Inspired by natural biophilic tones, warm organic paper surfaces, and breathable composition.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Combines warm ivory canvases, botanical greens, and soft ethereal accents (sky and lavender) to visually calm nervous systems.

### Visual Aesthetic
Minimalist organic warmth. The interface utilizes generous negative space, continuous soft curvature, subtle ambient light tiers, and flat minimalist vector art to eliminate cognitive friction and enhance clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, stress-relieving hierarchy anchored in deep botanical green and softened by warm daylight backgrounds.

### Color Hierarchy
- **Canvas & Base Surfaces:** Warm Cream (`#FFFDF9`) serves as the base layer, with Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`) for primary containers and structured cards.
- **Primary (Forest Vitality):** `#15803D` provides high-contrast reassurance for primary action buttons, focused states, and key navigational milestones. Paired with soft sage tints (`#DCFCE7` and `#F0FDF4`) for ambient highlights and subtle container fills.
- **Secondary (Serene Atmosphere):** Deep Sky (`#0284C7`) paired with Airy Mist (`#E0F2FE` and `#38BDF8`) for breathwork exercises, guided audio, and cognitive reflection modules.
- **Tertiary (Restorative Twilight):** Botanical Lavender (`#9333EA`, `#C084FC`, and `#F3E8FF`) applied purposefully for night routines, sleep aid modules, and creative emotional journaling.
- **Text & Contrast:** Deep Slate Brown (`#1E2922`) delivers legible, gentle contrast that softens the harshness of pure black `#000000`. Secondary body and supporting metadata use Muted Pine Slate (`#4A5A50`).

## Typography

The typography leverages Inter across all hierarchy tiers to maintain crisp readability, cognitive ease, and dependable clarity. 

- **Breathing Room:** Body copy uses increased line-height (`1.65` to `1.875`) to prevent eye fatigue during high-anxiety moments and prolonged self-reflection sessions.
- **Hierarchy Softening:** Display headings rely on semibold weights (`600`) with tight tracking rather than heavy black or ultra-bold weights, preserving an approachable, human presence.
- **Micro-Copy Clarity:** Meta tags, status chips, and breath interval indicators remain comfortably spaced with subtle tracking expansions to ensure instant legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture engineered with generous negative space to minimize visual noise and enhance emotional spaciousness.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout anchored with dynamic vertical padding. Bottom app safe-margins accommodate thumb-driven check-in rituals. Fluid margins start at `1.25rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Content cards divide into balanced 4-column groupings. Side sheet drawers preserve contextual spatial stability during journaling or exercise prompts.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed at 1160px container width. Centered reading modules and mindfulness flows avoid visual sprawl, maintaining comfortable eye-scanning tracks.

### Spacing Cadence
Components use a strict 4px/8px modular rhythm. Padding within interactive surfaces is biased toward relaxed, airy vertical spacing (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to prevent dense, spreadsheet-like interfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through translucent tonal stratification and gentle ambient light instead of heavy, sharp drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Palette
- **Low Elevation (`shadow-sm`):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 41, 34, 0.03)` with a soft, tinted border (`rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.08)`). Ideal for interactive cards and stateful chips.
- **Resting Focus (`shadow-md`):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 41, 34, 0.03)`. Reserved for featured mindfulness cards, active audio drawers, and daily wellness summaries.
- **Floating Modals (`shadow-lg`):** `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(30, 41, 34, 0.08)`. Tinted softly with canvas tones to maintain immersion without stark contrast breaks.

### Tonal Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas):** Warm Cream (`#FFFDF9`).
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border `rgba(220, 252, 231, 0.6)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules):** Soft Sage Wash (`#F0FDF4`) or Mist Blue (`#F0F9FF`) layered above primary containers.

## Shapes

The design language favors generous organic curvature, eliminating sharp angles to reduce visual tension and communicate a comforting presence.

- **Primary Containers & Modals:** Built with `rounded-3xl` (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) for a soft, pebble-like aesthetic.
- **Cards & Functional Tiles:** Styled with `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` to `1.25rem`).
- **Interactive Controls & Tags:** Buttons, mood selectors, and quick-filter chips utilize continuous pill shapes (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill shape, filled with Forest Vitality (`#15803D`), crisp white text, with comfortable vertical padding (`0.875rem 1.75rem`). Hover and focus trigger an ambient sage glow.
- **Secondary:** Warm ivory background with a 1px sage outline (`#DCFCE7`), text in `#15803D`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, muted slate pine text, subtly highlighting to `#F0FDF4` on interaction.

### Mood & Tag Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded (`0.5rem 1rem`), using soft pastel fills:
  - Sage (`#DCFCE7`) for grounding states.
  - Sky (`#E0F2FE`) for reflection and focus.
  - Lavender (`#F3E8FF`) for relaxation and bedtime check-ins.
- Active states are signified by a 1.5px solid border in the corresponding key hue.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F5` backgrounds, generous inner padding (`space-lg`), and `rounded-2xl` boundaries.
- Decorated with minimalist flat vector line art, calm botanical glyphs, or soft abstract organic gradients in the corner margins. No photographic imagery.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Generously rounded inputs (`rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`), warm ivory canvas fill (`#FAF8F5`), framed by subtle border tinting (`#DCFCE7`).
- Active focus smoothly highlights the container with an airy sage glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(220, 252, 231, 0.8)`) without aggressive color flips.

### Checkboxes, Switches & Radio Controls
- Radio check-ins resemble soft circular pebbles.
- Interactive switches utilize rounded pill tracks in Sage (`#DCFCE7`) with Forest Green thumb caps, creating a tactile and calming tactile response.

### Specialized App Components
- **Breathing Orbit Disc:** Centered radial scale component with layered concentric rings in Sky (`#E0F2FE`) and Sage (`#DCFCE7`) with gentle ease-in-out breathing rhythms.
- **Mood Check-in Matrix:** A horizontal row of organic rounded touch targets representing physical and emotional states, using gentle color transitions rather than jarring numerical grades.
- **Audio Reflection Player:** Low-profile dock with floating blur background (`backdrop-blur-md`), pill play/pause buttons, and a serene sky blue audio scrubber.
